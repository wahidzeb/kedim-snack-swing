About Kedim's Snack Swing

Kedim's Snack Swing is a rope-cutting physics puzzle starring a very hungry orange cat. Swipe the ropes, swing the fish treat, collect the stars and drop the treat into Kedim's mouth.

It starts easy and gets cleverer with every Snack Box, adding gadgets such as bubbles that float the treat upward. Every level can be solved, and a hint can replay the full solution.

3 quick tips

  1. Watch a full swing or two before touching anything. Many levels show you the answer.
  2. With two or three ropes, the order you cut them matters more than the timing.
  3. Restart freely. It costs nothing, and many levels take two or three tries to get the timing right.

How to Play

🐟 Feed Kedim! The fish treat hangs from ropes. Get it into Kedim's mouth.

✂️ Swipe across a rope to cut it. You can cut several ropes in one swipe.

⭐ Collect all 3 stars on the way. Stars unlock new snack boxes.

👆 Tap bubbles to pop them, air cushions to blow, and the gravity button to flip gravity.

💡 Stuck? A hint shows the full solution. Buy more hints with coins.

⌨️ Keyboard: drag with the mouse to cut · 1-9 cut ropes · Space pops the bubble · Q/E air cushions · G gravity · R restart · H hint · Esc pause
